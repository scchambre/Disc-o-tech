Click a row to plot it. reach ° = how the disc was tilted while held still in your reach-back. flight ° = the disc's actual spin-axis tilt in the air (measured from the gravity wobble — the truer release angle; needs a real spin). Both are total lean (hyzer + nose combined). lean (N) = compass direction the disc tips toward (in flight). vs line = that lean relative to your Throw line setting above (set it to your throwing direction; ↺180° for the opposite target). wobble ° = how far the spin wavers from perfectly steady (RMS degrees, sensor noise removed; lower = cleaner). RPM needs sample rate ≳ 2× spin frequency.
